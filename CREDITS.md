## Specific Contributions

### Spec Migrations System
- **Concept**: Arto Jalkanen - Event sourcing for specs, atomic operations for AI context windows
- **Hybrid Approach**: Tomas Günther & Arto Jalkanen - Complementary current-state and historical truth

### Dual License Model
- **GPL/Copyleft for Framework**: Arto Jalkanen - Ensuring framework improvements benefit the community
- **Implementation**: Tomas Günther - Three-tier licensing structure

### Design Principles
- **Green Coding**: Integrated throughout (token efficiency = computational efficiency)
- **Anti-Hallucination**: Research-backed rules for AI reliability
- **Theory of Fun**: Game development support inspired by Raph Koster and Jesse Schell
