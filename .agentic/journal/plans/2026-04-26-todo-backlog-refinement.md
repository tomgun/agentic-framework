## Verdict legend

- **KEEP** — bug fix or incremental work that is independent of v5; ship as planned regardless
- **MAP** — directly maps to a v5 backlog item (R-NNN); close the TODO and track via the v5 backlog instead
- **SUPERSEDED** — v5 mechanism replaces this entirely; close as obsolete
- **PARTIAL** — partially covered by v5; close most of it, keep a smaller scoped follow-up
- **DOWNGRADE** — was load-bearing pre-v5; v5 makes it lower-priority or curiosity-only
